What these numbers mean

Source: NTSB Table 5, 2005–2024. Rate = 17 ÷ 187,608,748 × 1,000,000. This is the pooled rate, not the mean of annual rates. Includes U.S. Part 121 passenger and cargo operations and fatalities on the ground. It describes recorded events per departure, not passenger survival probability. The source does not directly count completed safe journeys.
