Both charts show the same 2,792 records with a known airborne phase: departure, in flight, or arrival. The 82 ground records and 62 records without a phase remain in the retained source and are excluded from both plotted charts and their phase-share denominator. The ribbons partition accident records by their recorded phase; continuation means the accident was recorded later, not a measured successful flight. Each source record describes an aircraft; collisions may contribute more than one record.

The four causes are the archive’s broad classifications, not reviewed technical failure domains. Others and Not Recorded stay in the Sankey and source records. The area chart excludes them; Percentage uses the four displayed causes within each selected phase and period. Ribbon order is rank, thickness is count or share. Smooth crossings are visual interpolation. These records do not measure risk per flight or establish why safety changed.
