Data, definitions & prototype limits

Observed charts: PlaneCrashData by MauroPello, CC BY 4.0, downloaded October 23, 2026. Chart period: 1970–2025; 2026 is excluded as incomplete. These are recorded accidents, not a census of all flights. Coverage and classification may change over time.

Departures: World Bank / ICAO, indicator IS.AIR.DPRT, CC BY 4.0. Registered-carrier departures are shown as context, not automatically paired with a different accident population to estimate risk.

Fatality fraction pools onboard deaths over onboard occupants in records with complete, nonnegative, consistent counts and at least one occupant. Ground fatalities are excluded. Zero-occupancy, missing, and inconsistent records are excluded and counted.

Cause labels describe the source's classification; they are not independent causal findings. Technical subcategories use keyword mentions, with overlaps. Broad phase labels cannot distinguish takeoff from climb or approach from landing.

The dot field and success/failure flow use constructed examples, not measured aviation risk. No departure-rate estimate is made in this gallery.
